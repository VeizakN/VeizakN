# VeizakN

**Giving forgotten vanilla items a purpose, one small mod at a time.**

I make small, lightweight Minecraft mods focused on vanilla-friendly mechanics — usually by taking an overlooked item or block and giving it something useful to do.

## Minecraft Mods

### 🌟 Polestar
Use a vanilla Spyglass to find the North Star in the Overworld night sky. Center it in your lens to hear a quiet chime and briefly reveal a compass rose that rotates as you turn — no coordinates, minimap, or permanent HUD.

**Minecraft:** 26.2  
**Loader:** Fabric (client-side)  
**License:** MIT

[View on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/polestar)

---

### 🛡️ Golden Armor with Regen
Wear vanilla gold armor to gain passive Regeneration. The more pieces you equip, the stronger the effect becomes — up to Regeneration IV with a full set.

**Minecraft:** 1.12.2 / 1.20.1  
**Loader:** Forge  
**License:** MIT

[View on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/golden-armor-regen)

---

### 🔥 Eternal Furnace
Craft Hellfire Netherrack, place it beneath a regular Furnace, and get infinite fuel forever — at the cost of slower smelting.

**Loader:** Forge / NeoForge  
**License:** MIT

[View on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/eternal-furnace)

---

### 🕰️ Deadman's Clock
Adds the rare **Tidewatch** enchantment for the vanilla Clock. Carry an enchanted Clock to slow the natural passage of daytime by up to 4× without slowing the rest of the game.

**Minecraft:** 1.20.1  
**Loader:** Forge  
**License:** MIT

[View on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/deadman-s-clock)

## About my mods

- Lightweight, with no unnecessary dependencies where possible
- Singleplayer and multiplayer support when the mechanic allows it
